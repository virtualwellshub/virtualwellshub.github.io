SCHEDULE

Indoor pickleball in Woodstock is played during the hours listed below on a Pay as You Play basis ($9.00) or included with your Membership to the Woodstock Badminton Club

Paddles and balls provided. You can try it out FREE your first time!

 

 

2016 Membership Fee Schedule (Includes both Badminton & Pickleball)

Adult: $289 (18 and over)

Couple: $409 (2 adults 18 & over)

Friday Night Family: $299 (2 adults & unlimited children, Friday nights ONLY)

Family: $549 (2 adults & unlimited children)

Student: $189 (must be registered at an accredited institution up to age 25 or K-12)

Senior: $259 (age 55 & over)

Senior Couple: $369 (at least one person being age 55 & over)

 

Pickleball Play Times

Mondays: 7:00 pm to 9:00 pm

Tuesdays: 1:00 pm to 3:00 pm

Wednesdays: 9:00 am to 11:00 am

Wednesday Nights Outdoors: 6:30 pm to 8:30 pm

Fridays: 10:00 am to 12:00 pm

Saturdays: 3:00 pm to 5:00 pm

Note: Special events/touranments may pre-empt these times, please check the Woodstock Badminton Club Website for updated events and times.
