SCHEDULE

Indoor pickleball in Woodstock is played during the hours listed below on a Drop In Day Member basis ($10.00) OR included with your Membership to the Woodstock Badminton Club that allows you to play both Pickleball and Badminton anytime.

Paddles and pickleballs are available to use if you don't have your own equipment.

First time drop in members must pay a court fee and register for a Day Membership.

 

2024 Membership Fee Schedule (Includes Unlimited Badminton & Pickleball)

Adult: $370 (18 - 54 years)

Couple: $520 (2 adults 18 - 54 years)

Family: $700 (2 adults & unlimited children)

Youth: $220 (5-18 years)

Student: $255 (18-25 years registered full time post secondary)

Senior: $330 (age 55 & over)

Senior Couple: $470 (at least one person being age 55 & over)

 

 

Pickleball Play Times

Mondays: 9:00 am to 11:00 am

Mondays: 7:00 pm to 9:00 pm

Tuesdays: 1:00 pm to 3:00 pm

Wednesdays: 9:00 am to 11:00 am

Fridays: 9:00 am to 12:00 pm

Fridays: 7:00 pm to 9:00 pm

Saturdays: 3:00 pm to 5:00 pm

Sundays: 7:00 pm to 9:00 pm

 

Note: Special events/touranments may pre-empt these times, please check the Woodstock Badminton Club Website for updated events and times.
